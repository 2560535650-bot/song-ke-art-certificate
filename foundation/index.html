FOUNDATION PROFILE · 02

基金会简介

全球華人藝術基金會（GLOBAL CHINESE ART FOUNDATION）機構介紹

全球華人藝術基金會（GCAF），是在美國洛杉磯正式註冊成立的國際性非營利藝術機構，立足全球藝術視野，搭建東西方藝術對話的橋樑，致力於推動中華藝術的國際傳播、全球藝術資源整合與跨文化藝術交流。

作為扎根北美、輻射全球的專業藝術平台，基金會以開放包容的國際視角，打破地域與文化壁壘，聚焦東方當代藝術的當代表達與國際化轉譯。我們長期關注全球華人藝術家的成長與發展，為優秀藝術創作者提供國際展覽、學術研究、駐留創作、藏品推廣等全鏈路支持，助力根植東方文化的藝術語言走向世界藝術舞台。

基金會核心業務覆蓋國際藝術巡展、雙向藝術交流項目、青年藝術人才扶植、藝術學術研究出版、藝術衍生品與品牌跨界合作等領域。一方面引進國際頂尖藝術資源，促進中西藝術思想的碰撞與交融；另一方面系統梳理、推廣中國傳統美學與當代創作成果，讓蘊含東方哲思、華美意象的藝術作品，獲得全球範圍內的認知、認可與傳承。

在全球藝術格局多元發展的當下，GCAF堅持以藝術為通用語言，連結世界各地的藝術機構、美術館、策展人、藏家與大眾群體。通過專業化、國際化、系統化的運作模式，構建穩定持久的全球藝術交流網絡，既守護中華藝術的文化根脈，又賦予傳統藝術適配當代全球審美的表達形式，推動華人藝術在國際藝術體系中建立獨立的學術定位與市場影響力，最終實現東方藝術價值的全球共享與文明互鑒。

ENGLISH PROFILE

Global Chinese Art Foundation (GCAF)

Global Chinese Art Foundation (GCAF) is an international non‑profit art institution officially registered in Los Angeles, the United States. From a global artistic perspective, it builds a bridge for East‑West artistic dialogue, dedicated to promoting the international communication of Chinese art, integrating global art resources, and facilitating cross‑cultural art exchanges.

As a professional art platform rooted in North America and radiating across the world, the foundation breaks geographical and cultural barriers with an open and inclusive global vision, focusing on the contemporary expression and international translation of oriental art. We long‑term pay attention to the growth and development of Chinese artists worldwide, providing full‑chain support such as international exhibitions, academic research, residency creation, and collection promotion for outstanding creators, helping art rooted in oriental culture step onto the global art stage.

The foundation’s core businesses cover international art touring exhibitions, two‑way art exchange programs, support for young artists, academic research and publication, art derivatives and brand crossover cooperation. On one hand, it introduces top international art resources to promote the collision and integration of Chinese and Western artistic thoughts; on the other hand, it systematically sorts out and promotes traditional Chinese aesthetics and contemporary creative achievements, enabling art works containing oriental philosophy and gorgeous imagery to gain recognition and inheritance worldwide.

In the era of diversified development of the global art landscape, GCAF adheres to art as a universal language, connecting art institutions, museums, curators, collectors and the general public around the world. Through professional, international and systematic operation mode, it builds a stable and long‑term global art exchange network. While preserving the cultural roots of Chinese art, it endows traditional art with forms of expression adapted to contemporary global aesthetics, promotes Chinese art to establish independent academic orientation and market influence in the international art system, and finally realizes the global sharing of oriental artistic value and mutual learning of civilizations.
